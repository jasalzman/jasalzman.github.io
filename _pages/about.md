I am a PhD candidate at the University of Texas at Austin department of Government studying under Dr. Daron Shaw. My research interests include:

## Research Interests 
- Campaigns and elections, with a focus on state and local elections
- State and local politics
- Urban/rural Politics
- The role of religious institutions in campaigns and state/local governance

## Dissertation 
My dissertation explores how nationalization impacts campaign ads and communications at the local and state level. I investigate what types of campaigns used nationalized messaging as well as how voters respond to these messages.
