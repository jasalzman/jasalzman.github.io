Conjoint experiments randomize each attribute independently, so they recover only controlled effects. They can't show how one attribute works through another, such as how a candidate's traits operate through perceived party. This paper develops a framework that adds one simple experiment to measure those relationships. It combines that experiment with standard causal mediation assumptions and estimates the effects using doubly robust machine learning. We demonstrate the approach with a preregistered candidate-choice experiment.
